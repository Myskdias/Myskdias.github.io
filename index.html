01 / Research

Learning from structured experience.

Current research2026

Shandess

Student history meets language models.

How can a language model use a long history of student interactions without processing it as a long text prompt?

I design history encoders that turn structured interactions into compact representations for a language model. The goal is to support knowledge tracing and multiple-choice answer prediction, while keeping inference efficient.

History encodingLanguage modelsStudent modeling
More about the research

I explore soft prompts and cross-attention mechanisms for injecting student histories into a language model, and evaluate how predictive performance changes with the amount of available history.

This work is part of the MIAAM project at Inria Flowers, supervised by Pierre-Yves Oudeyer and Loris Gaven.
